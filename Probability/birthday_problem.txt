Question : If there are n people in a room, how large 
must n be for there to be at least a 50% chance that at 
least 2 people share a birthday?

Instead of Directly calculating -> P(at least 2 people share)

calculating P(at least 2 people share) is difficult because there are many possible ways people 
can share birthdays.

so, we can just calculate the opposite event -> P(no one shares)

Then:
P(at least 2 share) = 1-P(no one shares)

Solution:
person 1 can have any of the days in 365 days
so, p(first person's birthday) = 365/365
p(second person's birthday) = 364/365 (because person one took one of the days)
...
...


so, P(no one shares)=365/365​×364​/365×363/365​​×....×365−n+1​/365

why multiplied -> what is the probability of two things must happen, AND -> p(aub) = p(a)p(b/a)

so, p(no one shares) = 365!/(365 -n)!365^n

now for p(atlest 2 share) = 1 - 365!/(365 -n)!365^n

so, here for only n = 23, the p(atleast 2 shares) is near to 50%

so, the n = 23 is the correct answer
